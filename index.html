About MaiML

MaiML (Measurement Analysis Instrument Markup Language) is an open XML-based format for describing measurement and analysis workflows together with samples, experimental conditions, results, provenance, and related metadata.

The MaiML ecosystem provides specifications, XML schemas, software libraries, developer tools, and end-user applications to improve interoperability, reproducibility, traceability, and long-term data utilization.

Standards

The specification and requirements of MaiML are defined by the standards below. The Japanese Industrial Standard was established in 2024; the international standard is currently under development.

Established

JIS K 0200:2024

Measurement analysis data format (MaiML), established as a Japanese Industrial Standard in 2024. It defines how the structure, identification, relations and history of measurement and analysis data are described.

  • Established— 2024
  • Published by— Japanese Standards Association
In development

ISO standard

The international standard is under development: a working draft is being prepared ahead of a new work item proposal. It is a proposal independent of the existing JIS.

  • Status— under development
  • Stage— working draft, proposal in preparation

Operational Guideline

Recommended operational procedures for connecting the identification, structure, relations, provenance and signatures provided by MaiML to UUID issuance, key management, access control, preservation, discovery and vocabulary management. Informative: it adds no conformity requirements to the standard text or the XML schema.

Draft 0.8.14

MaiML Operational Guideline

Applies to the MaiML Working Draft and schema release 1.1.0. The English edition is a working translation that has not been independently approved.

  • English edition— Markdown edition v0.8.14 (working translation)
  • Japanese edition— Markdown edition v0.8.14

Manifesto

A stand-alone document stating the design principles behind MaiML as a ten-article manifesto. It is non-normative and a pre-approval draft; the specification and its requirements are defined by JIS K 0200 and the MaiML schema.

Draft 1.0.0

The MaiML Manifesto

Design principles for research as a knowledge-generation process: the preamble, ten articles and creed, the rationale for each principle, and their mapping to the structure of MaiML.

  • English edition— Markdown edition v1.0.0
  • Japanese edition— Markdown edition v1.0.0
